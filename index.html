Artist Profile

The
Story

Darwin Rivera — known as BlackDarkwin — is a Uruguayan composer, producer, and multi-instrumentalist who inhabits a rare duality: creating both Cinematic/Orchestral music and Commercial Pop/Electronic music with the same technical rigor and emotional intensity.

Trained in piano, violin, and voice from an early age, he refined his musical language at the University School of Music and as part of the Symphonic Band and Philharmonic Orchestra of Montevideo.

A turning point came in 2017, when his connection with composer Mark Petrie introduced him to the Los Angeles audiovisual scene, leading to work on campaigns for Netflix productions such as The Old Guard, The Midnight Sky, and Blue Miracle.

At the core of his work is human emotion — his music speaks of hope and transformation, seeking to be a sonic refuge where listeners can recognize themselves.
